## Expected RAG Behaviour

For each question, the system should:

- Retrieve relevant authoritative documents.
- Identify the relevant section, rule, or guideline.
- Give an answer supported by the retrieved evidence.
- Provide a citation to the supporting source.
- Avoid treating international WIPO material as Indian domestic law.
- Avoid giving unsupported legal conclusions.
- Clearly distinguish between statutory law, official guidelines, manuals, and international reference material.
