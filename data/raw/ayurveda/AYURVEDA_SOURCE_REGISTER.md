## Data Collection Rules

Only record information that can be traced to an authoritative source.

Do not create synthetic traditional knowledge.

Do not invent:

- Ayurvedic uses
- Formulations
- Sanskrit verses
- Plant properties
- Traditional claims
- Community knowledge

Every dataset record must have a source reference.

---

## Access and Licensing

The dataset should distinguish between:

- Publicly available information
- Authorized information
- Restricted information
- Information requiring permission or licensing

Restricted databases should not be scraped or copied without
authorization.

The full TKDL database must not be treated as a freely downloadable
public dataset.

---

## Initial Dataset Goal

Build a small, high-quality starter dataset.

Priority categories:

1. Medicinal plants
2. Ayurvedic names
3. Botanical names
4. Vernacular names
5. Traditional-use information
6. Formulations
7. Source texts
8. References

Quality and traceability are more important than dataset size.
